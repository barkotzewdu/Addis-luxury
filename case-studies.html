These case studies represent real client experiences from our Addis Ababa and Dubai ateliers (names withheld for privacy). Each story follows our core philosophy of listening to the challenge, designing a thoughtful solution, and delivering an impeccable result.

Bridal

A Wedding Dress That Honors Two Cultures

The Challenge

A bride based in London with Ethiopian and Emirati heritage wanted a wedding dress that honored both cultures without feeling like a costume. She needed a gown suitable for both a church ceremony and an evening reception, with elements that would be recognized and appreciated by family from both backgrounds.

The Solution

Our design team created a two-look concept from a single dress. The primary silhouette featured clean, structured couture lines with a detachable hand-embroidered Ethiopian netela-inspired cape. The embroidery incorporated traditional Ethiopian cross patterns rendered in contrasting thread on ivory silk, a detail that resonated with both cultures' love of geometric beauty.

The Result

The bride wore the cape for the ceremony, then removed it for a streamlined reception look. Family members from both sides commented on how the design felt authentic to their traditions while being entirely original.

Corporate

Executive Wardrobe for an International Role

The Challenge

A senior executive relocating from Addis Ababa to Dubai needed to rebuild a professional wardrobe suited to a new climate and business culture while maintaining a personal style rooted in Ethiopian aesthetics.

The Solution

We designed a capsule wardrobe of 6 pieces: 3 lightweight wool suits in earth tones, 2 structured blazers with subtle Ethiopian textile linings visible only at the cuff, and 1 evening suit with hand-stitched lapel details. Fabrics were selected for Dubai's climate while maintaining the structured appearance expected in corporate settings.

The Result

The client reported feeling confident and authentic in business meetings across the Gulf region. The hidden textile details became conversation starters that naturally introduced her heritage in professional contexts.

Special Occasion

A Gala Gown with Meaning

The Challenge

A client attending an international charity gala in Dubai wanted a statement gown that would stand out among designer labels while telling a story of cultural pride and social impact.

The Solution

We created a floor-length gown using hand-spun Ethiopian cotton as the base fabric, dyed in deep indigo, with a structured bodice following Arabian couture construction methods. The skirt panels incorporated hand-woven strips from a women's cooperative in rural Ethiopia, creating a textural mosaic effect.

The Result

The gown drew attention from fashion commentators at the event. More importantly, the story of the women's cooperative behind the fabric generated interest in the client's philanthropic work, turning a fashion moment into an advocacy opportunity.

Heritage

Preserving a Family Textile Legacy

The Challenge

A client inherited hand-woven fabrics from her grandmother, a renowned weaver in the Dorze community of southern Ethiopia. She wanted to honor this legacy by having the fabrics made into wearable garments without cutting or altering the original weaving.

The Solution

Our team worked with the client over several months to design garments that used the inherited textiles as featured panels within modern constructions. Each piece was built around the dimensions of the original weaving, using complementary new fabrics to frame and protect the heirloom textiles. Reinforcement techniques ensured the delicate vintage fabric would withstand regular wear.

The Result

Three garments were created: a structured jacket, an evening wrap, and a display piece suitable for framing when not worn. The client now wears her grandmother's artistry to family gatherings and cultural events, keeping the weaving tradition alive in a new form.
