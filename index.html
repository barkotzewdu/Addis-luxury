Our Narrative

A garment begins long before the needle meets the cloth.

In the highlands of Ethiopia, silk is still spun and loomed by hand — thread by patient thread — by weavers whose families have practised the craft for generations. The cloth carries the memory of the land it came from.

In our ateliers, master pattern cutters approach a garment as architecture, drafting, fitting, and refitting until the silhouette belongs entirely to its wearer. Two or three fittings are never an inconvenience; they are the point.
