Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Addis Luxury. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and bespoke fashion services. By accessing our website or utilizing our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information in connection with the services we provide, including:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details provided voluntarily via inquiry forms, consultation bookings, or email correspondence.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you navigate and interact with our website, collected through analytics tools.
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device to enhance site functionality and track website performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To provide, maintain, and improve our website and services.
  • To send you promotional communications and updates, only with your explicit consent.
  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior for optimization purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve user experience. We use essential cookies required for basic site functionality and analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with our site. You can manage your cookie preferences through the cookie consent banner displayed upon your first visit, or by adjusting your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We utilize third-party services that may collect, process, or store data on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymized usage data to help us analyze website traffic.
  • Google Maps: Used to display our atelier locations.
  • Google Fonts: Provides typography for our website.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Upon request, or when data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize your personal information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • GDPR Rights: The right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing.
  • CCPA Rights: The right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt-out of the sale of personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 12.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

As an international brand with ateliers in Ethiopia and the UAE, your information may be processed and stored in these locations or in other countries where our service providers operate. By using our services, you consent to the transfer of your data to these regions.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
