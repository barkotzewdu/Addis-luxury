Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

These Terms and Conditions govern your use of the Addis Luxury website and our bespoke fashion services. By engaging with our services or accessing our website, you agree to be bound by these terms. Addis Luxury operates as a premium fashion house with ateliers in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and Dubai, UAE.

2. Services

Addis Luxury offers bespoke, made-to-measure, and ready-to-wear luxury fashion items. The bespoke process involves initial consultations, design proposals, fabric selection, and multiple fittings to ensure a flawless final garment.

3. Custom Orders

When placing a custom order with Addis Luxury, the following terms apply:

  • Order Placement: Orders are confirmed only after design approval and receipt of the initial deposit.
  • Design Approval: Clients must sign off on sketches, fabric choices, and design details before production commences.
  • Client Responsibilities: Clients must attend scheduled fittings and provide accurate measurements when requested. Failure to do so may result in delays or additional charges.

4. Pricing and Payment

All financial transactions are subject to the following terms:

  • Quotes: Provided quotes remain valid for 30 days from the date of issue.
  • Deposits: A non-refundable 50% deposit is required to initiate any bespoke or custom order.
  • Balance Due: The remaining 50% balance must be settled in full prior to the final delivery of the garment.
  • Payment Methods: We accept bank transfers, major credit cards, and specialized financing options where applicable. Payments can be processed in USD or AED.

5. Fitting and Delivery

Fittings are essential to the bespoke process. We require client cooperation to adhere to the fitting schedule. While we provide estimated delivery timelines, these are not guaranteed due to the intricate nature of bespoke craftsmanship. For international orders, shipping terms, costs, and applicable customs duties will be clearly communicated and borne by the client.

6. Alterations and Adjustments

Standard alterations necessary to achieve the agreed-upon design are included within the initial fitting process. Any significant design changes requested after initial approval or post-delivery alterations (e.g., due to weight fluctuations) will incur additional costs, which will be quoted prior to the work being carried out.

7. Cancellations and Refunds

Our cancellation and refund policy is as follows:

  • Before Production: Cancellations made before production begins will receive a full refund, minus any consultation or design fees incurred.
  • During Production: Cancellations made while production is underway will receive a partial refund, prorated based on the work already completed.
  • Post-Approval: No refunds are issued after the final fitting and client approval.
  • Non-Refundable Items: Custom fabrics, specialized embellishments, or materials ordered specifically for your garment are strictly non-refundable.
