The Visionary

The House begins with a belief.

ADISS was conceived around a simple conviction: that clothing should express something deeper than the moment. This is the story of the people who hold that conviction, and of the House they are building around it.

Read the Story ↓

The Conviction

Dress the person you intend to become.

The idea for ADISS did not begin with a garment. It began with a question about identity: what does the way we dress say about who we are, and who we are becoming?

The answer became the philosophy of the House. Clothing is more than what the world sees. It is a reflection of what we believe, what we pursue, and who we choose to become. ADISS exists to give that conviction a form.

The ambition is to build a House with a distinctive design language, a meaningful cultural perspective, and an enduring relationship with the people who wear its creations.

The Custodians

Custodians of the House.

The House is held by two custodians, each responsible for a different dimension of its character.

Barkot Zewdu

Architecture & Digital Systems

Barkot gives the House its structure — the systems, the digital architecture and the quiet interfaces through which ADISS is encountered. His work is measured, not decorative.

Elias Yonas

Vision & Brand Direction

Elias holds the vision of the House — its silhouette, its voice and the discipline of restraint that guides every collection and private commission.

A Living Vision

A House measured in decades, not seasons.

ADISS is still becoming. The House is being built slowly, with the belief that the most meaningful things are understood over time: a silhouette, a material, a standard, a relationship.

The vision is not simply to make more clothing. It is to create a world in which dressing, thinking, and becoming are understood as part of the same act.
