How It Works

Your bespoke journey in four steps.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Book a private appointment at either atelier or schedule a virtual consultation. Share your vision, occasion, and style preferences.

  2. 02

    Design & Fabric

    Our designers sketch concepts based on your brief. Browse our curated fabric library and select materials that speak to you.

  3. 03

    Fitting & Refinement

    Try your garment as it takes shape. Each fitting allows us to perfect the fit, adjust details, and ensure your complete satisfaction.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Your finished piece, pressed and packaged in our signature presentation, ready to wear.
