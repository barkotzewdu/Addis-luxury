Our Story

Two cultures. One shared passion for craftsmanship. This is how Addis Luxury began.

An Extraordinary Partnership

Addis Luxury was born from a chance meeting at a textile exhibition in Istanbul in 2019. Two designers from different corners of the world discovered they shared an identical philosophy: that luxury fashion should honor its origins.

From Addis Ababa, came a deep understanding of Ethiopia's ancient weaving traditions, the art of hand-spun cotton, and the geometric precision of traditional Habesha patterns. From Dubai, came mastery of couture construction, an understanding of the global luxury market, and access to the finest fabrics from European mills.

Together, they founded Addis Luxury with a clear mission: create bespoke garments that carry the soul of Ethiopian craftsmanship, finished with the precision of Arabian couture.

Meet the Founders

Barkot Zewdu

Co-Founder & Creative Director, Addis Ababa

Growing up around the vibrant textile communities of Addis Ababa, Barkot learned traditional weaving from family elders before formalizing his education with a degree in Fashion Design.

His lifelong ambition has been to bring the distinct beauty of Ethiopian patterns and hand-spun materials to the global stage, combining heritage techniques with modern silhouettes.

At Addis Luxury, Barkot is the driving force behind preserving these ancient crafts. His passion ensures that every collection honors the artisans' skill while innovating the way traditional textiles are worn today.

Elias Yonas

Co-Founder & Design Director, Dubai

Raised in Dubai's dynamic and cosmopolitan fashion scene, Elias sought formal training in Paris and Milan, mastering the intricacies of haute couture and precision tailoring.

Having spent over a decade working within renowned luxury fashion houses, Elias developed a profound appreciation for cross-cultural collaboration, flawless construction, and sharp business acumen.

At Addis Luxury, he merges his sophisticated understanding of the modern luxury client with Barkot's heritage materials, culminating in bespoke garments that are meticulously tailored and globally resonant.

Our Craftsmanship Philosophy

Heritage First

Every garment we produce is rooted in honoring the traditional techniques that have been passed down through generations. From the meticulous hand-spinning of cotton threads to the intricate, geometric patterns woven on traditional looms, we preserve the soul of Ethiopian craftsmanship. We refuse to compromise on authenticity, ensuring that the legacy of these ancient skills remains alive in every stitch.

Personal Connection

We believe that true luxury lies in the deeply personal relationship between the artisan and the wearer. Our bespoke client experience is defined by collaboration, dialogue, and intimacy. From the initial consultation to the final fitting, our clients are integral to the creative process, ensuring that the finished piece is a profound expression of their individual style and story, crafted exclusively for them.

Sustainable Luxury

Our commitment to excellence extends to the communities that make our work possible. We source ethically and ensure that our artisans receive fair wages that reflect their mastery. Environmental care is paramount, which is why we emphasize slow fashion, enduring quality, and environmentally conscious materials. We create heirloom pieces designed to be cherished for a lifetime, not seasonal trends.

Inside Our Ateliers

The Addis Ababa Atelier

Nestled near Bole Road, our flagship atelier is the pulsing heart of Addis Luxury's heritage. Here, the rhythmic clack of traditional wooden looms provides the soundtrack to our creative process. Clients visiting this space can witness firsthand the mesmerizing transformation of raw, hand-spun cotton into intricate textiles.

The atmosphere is one of focused artistry and deep respect for tradition. Surrounded by vibrant threads and heritage patterns, you are invited to collaborate directly with the master weavers and tailors, ensuring your bespoke piece captures the genuine spirit of Ethiopia.

The Dubai Atelier

Located in the prestigious Fashion Avenue of The Dubai Mall, our Dubai atelier represents the sophisticated, global face of Addis Luxury. This space is a sanctuary of modern elegance, designed for the utmost privacy and personalized service. It houses our extensive library of premium European fabrics and serves as our hub for precision couture construction.

Clients experience a seamless blend of luxurious hospitality and exacting sartorial standards. During a fitting, our expert pattern makers and style advisors dedicate themselves to achieving the perfect cut and silhouette, ensuring a flawless finish for every occasion.
