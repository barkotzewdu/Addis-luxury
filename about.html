The House of ADISS

Dress The Person You Intend To Become.

Clothing is more than what the world sees. It is a reflection of what you believe, what you pursue, and who you choose to become. ADISS was conceived around this conviction: that what we wear should express something deeper than the moment.

Discover the House ↓

The Belief

We believe clothing should reflect intention.

Before a garment becomes part of a wardrobe, it becomes part of a presence. The right form, the right material, and the smallest considered detail can change how a person carries themselves. We approach clothing as an expression of character — designed not simply for the person you are today, but for the person you intend to become.

Our Point of View

A distinct perspective. An enduring purpose.

Heritage, understood deeply.

Heritage is not a costume or a reference to the past. It is a living source of knowledge, identity, and design. ADISS draws inspiration from the depth of Ethiopian cultural expression, approaching its details with curiosity, respect, and the intention to give them a considered place in contemporary luxury.

Craft, treated with intention.

True refinement is found in decisions that may never demand attention: the behaviour of a fabric, the balance of a silhouette, the precision of a finish, and the way a garment lives with its wearer. We believe every design decision should have a reason to exist.

Progress, without losing character.

Innovation matters when it serves the experience. We look toward a future in which thoughtful design, advanced tools, and a more personal understanding of the client can coexist with the human judgment and care that make clothing meaningful.

The ADISS Code

The ADISS Code

A House is defined not only by what it creates, but by the standards behind every decision.

01

Intention

Nothing should exist merely to follow a trend. Every design begins with a reason.

02

Character

Clothing should express the person wearing it, not erase their individuality.

03

Permanence

We pursue relevance beyond the season: thoughtful design, considered materials, and details that reward attention.

04

Discovery

The most meaningful details do not always announce themselves. Some are understood gradually, through closer attention and personal connection.

Material, Form, and Detail

Considered in every detail.

A garment is understood through more than its appearance. It is understood through its weight, movement, texture, construction, and relationship with the body. At ADISS, our ambition is to bring these elements into a coherent whole — where form serves character, materials contribute to the experience, and details reveal their purpose over time.

The People Behind the House

A vision in the making.

ADISS began with a conviction about the relationship between clothing and identity. The ambition is to build a House with a distinctive design language, a meaningful cultural perspective, and an enduring relationship with the people who wear its creations.

The Future of the House

Built with intention. Looking beyond the moment.

We see ADISS as a House that should grow without losing its character. A House where cultural depth and contemporary expression can coexist. Where innovation supports the experience rather than distracts from it. And where every decision is measured against a single standard: to build something that deserves to remain.

The ambition is not simply to make more clothing. It is to build a world in which dressing, thinking, and becoming are understood as part of the same act.
